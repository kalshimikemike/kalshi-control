🧠 Learn the market

1. What does 20¢ mean?
A 20¢ contract costs $0.20. If it wins, it pays $1 before fees. If it loses, you lose the $0.20. Cheap does not mean undervalued.
2. What does 83¢ mean?
An 83¢ contract costs $0.83 and pays $1 if correct. Its gross profit is $0.17 per contract before fees. Price is not a guaranteed probability.
3. What is momentum?
Momentum measures how quickly an underlying asset is moving. A sudden reversal can invalidate an earlier directional signal.
4. Why check the order book?
The displayed price is not necessarily the price at which you can buy or sell your desired quantity. Depth and spread matter.
5. Why NO TRADE?
Mixed signals, wide spreads, missing data or poor exit liquidity can make a trade unattractive. Skipping is a valid decision.
