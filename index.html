What the controls mean

Bankroll: the imaginary funds used by this simulator. Risk per trade: maximum amount allocated to one simulated position, not a guaranteed maximum loss in live trading. Daily loss stop: pauses new paper entries after realized daily losses reach the configured threshold. Kill switch: blocks new simulated entries; it does not close existing positions.
